# Summer Holidays

Summer (夏, *natsu*) is one of the liveliest seasons in Japan, filled with festivals, fireworks, and trips to the sea. This lesson covers vocabulary for the summer break, seasonal events, and the things you eat and do to stay cool.

## Summer Season Basics

| Hiragana | Kanji | Romaji | English | Type |
|----------|-------|--------|---------|------|
| なつ | 夏 | natsu | ☀️ summer | noun |
| なつやすみ | 夏休み | natsuyasumi | 🏖️ summer holidays | noun |
| きゅうか | 休暇 | kyuuka | 🗓️ leave, vacation | noun |
| おぼん | お盆 | obon | 🏮 Obon (festival of the dead) | noun |
| きせつ | 季節 | kisetsu | 🍃 season | noun |
| なつばて | 夏バテ | natsubate | 🥵 summer fatigue | noun |
| ひやけ | 日焼け | hiyake | 🌞 suntan, sunburn | noun |

**Examples:**

- なつやすみは いなかに かえります。 — I go back to the countryside for the summer holidays.
  - *natsuyasumi wa inaka ni kaerimasu.*
- おぼんに かぞくが あつまります。 — The family gathers for Obon.
  - *obon ni kazoku ga atsumarimasu.*

## Talking About Your Trip

Back from the break, everyone swaps stories about where they went. Many people head south (みなみ) for warmer seas and sunny weather.

| Hiragana | Kanji | Romaji | English | Type |
|----------|-------|--------|---------|------|
| りょこう | 旅行 | ryokou | 🧳 trip, travel | noun |
| みなみ | 南 | minami | 🧭 south | noun |
| おもいで | 思い出 | omoide | 💭 memory | noun |
| おとずれる | 訪れる | otozureru | to visit | verb |
| たのしむ | 楽しむ | tanoshimu | to enjoy | verb |
| くつろぐ | - | kutsurogu | to relax, to unwind | verb |

**Examples:**

- なつやすみに みなみの しまを おとずれました。 — I visited a southern island during the summer holidays.
  - *natsuyasumi ni minami no shima o otozuremashita.*
- うみで およいで、アイスクリームを たべました。 — I swam in the sea and ate ice cream.
  - *umi de oyoide, aisukuriimu o tabemashita.*
- とても たのしかったです。 — It was a lot of fun.
  - *totemo tanoshikatta desu.*
- ゆっくり くつろげて よかったです。 — It was nice to relax and take it easy.
  - *yukkuri kutsurogete yokatta desu.*

## Useful Expressions

### Asking About Someone's Plans

- Q: なつやすみは なにを しますか。 — What are you doing for the summer holidays?
  - *natsuyasumi wa nani o shimasu ka.*
- A: うみに いく つもりです。 — I plan to go to the sea.
  - *umi ni iku tsumori desu.*

- Q: どこか いきましたか。 — Did you go anywhere?
  - *dokoka ikimashita ka.*
- A: おきなわで かいすいよくを しました。 — I went swimming at the beach in Okinawa.
  - *okinawa de kaisuiyoku o shimashita.*

:::tip Common Phrases
- あついですね (atsui desu ne) — It's hot, isn't it?
  - The go-to summer small talk, used constantly in July and August
- おげんきですか、なつバテに きをつけて (ogenki desu ka, natsubate ni ki o tsukete) — How are you? Take care not to get worn out by the heat
  - A warm sign-off in summer letters and messages
- よい なつやすみを！ (yoi natsuyasumi o!) — Have a good summer holiday!
  - Said before the break begins
:::
