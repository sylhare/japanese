# Beach and Holidays

Summer in Japan means heading to the **うみ** (sea) for a day of swimming, snacks, and sandcastles. This lesson covers vocabulary for the beach setting, the gear you bring, seaside activities, festival-style food, and the nuisances best avoided.

## At the Beach

| Hiragana | Kanji | Romaji | English | Type |
|----------|-------|--------|---------|------|
| ビーチ | - | biichi | 🏖️ beach | noun |
| うみ | 海 | umi | sea, ocean | noun |
| かいがん | 海岸 | kaigan | 🏝️ coast, seashore | noun |
| すな | 砂 | suna | sand | noun |
| なみ | 波 | nami | 🌊 wave | noun |
| たいよう | 太陽 | taiyou | ☀️ sun | noun |
| ひざし | 日差し | hizashi | 🌅 sunlight, sun's rays | noun |
| かい | 貝 | kai | 🐚 seashell | noun |
| すなの しろ | 砂の城 | suna no shiro | 🏰 sandcastle | noun |
| あつい | 暑い | atsui | hot (weather) | い-adjective |

**Examples:**

- うみで すなの しろを つくりました。 — I built a sandcastle at the sea.
  - *umi de suna no shiro o tsukurimashita.*
- なみが たかいですね。 — The waves are high, aren't they?
  - *nami ga takai desu ne.*

For broader nature words (やま, かわ, みずうみ) see the [Travel and Tourism](./travel) article, and for seasons and temperature (なつ, さむい, おんど) see [Weather](./weather).
:::

## Beach Gear

| Hiragana | Kanji | Romaji | English | Type |
|----------|-------|--------|---------|------|
| みずぎ | 水着 | mizugi | 🩱 swimsuit | noun |
| タオル | - | taoru | 🧺 towel | noun |
| ビーチパラソル | - | biichi parasoru | ⛱️ beach umbrella | noun |
| ひやけどめ | 日焼け止め | hiyakedome | 🧴 sunscreen | noun |
| サングラス | - | sangurasu | 🕶️ sunglasses | noun |
| ぼうし | 帽子 | boushi | 👒 hat | noun |
| うきわ | 浮き輪 | ukiwa | 🛟 swim ring, float | noun |
| バケツ | - | baketsu | 🪣 bucket | noun |

**Examples:**

- ひやけどめを ぬるのを わすれないでね。 — Don't forget to put on sunscreen.
  - *hiyakedome o nuru no o wasurenaide ne.*
- あたらしい みずぎを かいました。 — I bought a new swimsuit.
  - *atarashii mizugi o kaimashita.*
- ひやけどめを ぬった？ — Did you put on sunscreen?
  - *hiyakedome o nutta?*
- パラソルを かりられますか。 — Can I rent a parasol?
  - *parasoru o kariraremasu ka.*
- はい、うみのいえで かりられますよ。 — Yes, you can rent one at the beach hut.
  - *hai, umi no ie de kariraremasu yo.*

## Beach Activities

| Hiragana | Kanji | Romaji | English | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| すいえい | 水泳 | suiei | 🏊 swimming | noun |
| サーフィン | - | saafin | 🏄 surfing | noun |
| シュノーケリング | - | shunookeringu | 🤿 snorkeling | noun |
| ビーチボール | - | biichi booru | 🏐 beach ball | noun |
| ふね | 船 | fune | 🚣 boat | noun |
| たのしい | 楽しい | tanoshii | 😄 fun, enjoyable | い-adjective |

:::note Sunbathing and Beach Fun
Relaxing in the sun is called **日向ぼっこ (ひなたぼっこ, hinatabokko)** (*sunbathing / basking in the sun*).
:::

**Examples:**

- ともだちと すいかわりを しました。 — I played watermelon splitting with my friends.
  - *tomodachi to suikawari o shimashita.*
- いっしょに およぎませんか。 — Won't you swim with me?
  - *issho ni oyogimasen ka.*
- いいですね、いきましょう！ — Sounds good, let's go!
  - *ii desu ne, ikimashou!*

:::tip スイカ割り (Watermelon Splitting)
A classic summer beach game: blindfolded, spun around, and handed a stick, you try to smash a watermelon while friends shout directions. Afterwards everyone eats it — practical *and* fun!
:::

## Verbs

| Hiragana | Kanji | Romaji | English | Type |
|----------|-------|--------|---------|------|
| およぐ | 泳ぐ | oyogu | to swim | verb |
| もぐる | 潜る | moguru | to dive, to go underwater | verb |
| あそぶ | 遊ぶ | asobu | to play, to have fun | verb |
| ひやけする | 日焼けする | hiyake suru | to get a suntan, to get sunburnt | verb |
| おぼれる | 溺れる | oboreru | to drown | verb |
| ながれる | 流れる | nagareru | to drift, to be swept along | verb |

**Examples:**

- うみで いちにちじゅう およぎました。 — I swam in the sea all day.
  - *umi de ichinichijuu oyogimashita.*
- ふかい ところで およがない ほうが いいですよ。 — You shouldn't swim in deep places.
  - *fukai tokoro de oyoganai hou ga ii desu yo.*

## Food and Drinks

| Hiragana | Kanji | Romaji | English | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| かきごおり | かき氷 | kakigoori | 🍧 shaved ice | noun |
| アイスクリーム | - | aisukuriimu | 🍦 ice cream | noun |
| やきそば | 焼きそば | yakisoba | 🍜 fried noodles | noun |
| やきとうもろこし | 焼きとうもろこし | yaki toumorokoshi | 🌽 grilled corn | noun |
| スイカ | 西瓜 | suika | 🍉 watermelon | noun |
| ジュース | - | juusu | 🧃 juice | noun |

:::note 海の家 (Umi no Ie - Beach Huts)
**海の家 (うみのいえ, umi no ie)** are seasonal seaside shacks open during summer months. They provide food, drinks, rental gear, and changing rooms right on the sand.
:::

**Examples:**

- うみのいえで かきごおりを たべました。 — I ate shaved ice at the beach hut.
  - *umi no ie de kakigoori o tabemashita.*
- あついから、つめたい ジュースが のみたいです。 — Because it's hot, I want to drink a cold juice.
  - *atsui kara, tsumetai juusu ga nomitai desu.*

:::tip 海の家 (Umi no Ie)
The **海の家** are temporary huts that pop up on Japanese beaches every summer, selling snacks like やきそば and かきごおり and renting out parasols and floats. They vanish again once the season ends.
:::

## Nuisances and Safety

| Hiragana | Kanji | Romaji | English | Type |
|----------|-------|--------|---------|------|
| か | 蚊 | ka | 🦟 mosquito | noun |
| むしさされ | 虫刺され | mushisasare | 🩹 insect bite | noun |
| くらげ | - | kurage | 🪼 jellyfish | noun |
| ひやけ | 日焼け | hiyake | 🔴 sunburn, suntan | noun |
| ねっちゅうしょう | 熱中症 | necchuushou | 🥵 heatstroke | noun |
| むしよけ | 虫除け | mushiyoke | 🧴 insect repellent | noun |

**Examples:**

- かに さされました。 — I got bitten by a mosquito.
  - *ka ni sasaremashita.*
- みずを たくさん のんで、ねっちゅうしょうに きを つけて。 — Drink plenty of water and watch out for heatstroke.
  - *mizu o takusan nonde, necchuushou ni ki o tsukete.*
- あそこは ながれが つよいから、きを つけて。 — The current is strong over there, so be careful.
  - *asoko wa nagare ga tsuyoi kara, ki o tsukete.*

:::warning くらげ (Jellyfish) Season
Jellyfish become common on Japanese beaches from mid-August (after お盆, Obon). Locals often say once お盆 passes, it's time to stop swimming in the sea.
:::
