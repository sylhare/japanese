# Giving Advice: ほう が いい

Learn how to give advice and recommendations using **ほう が いい** ("it's better to..." / "you should...").

## Key Expressions

| Hiragana | Romaji | English | Type |
|----------|--------|---------|------|
| [verb た] + ほう が いい です | [ta-form] hou ga ii desu | you should (do) | expression |
| [verb ない] + ほう が いい です | [nai-form] hou ga ii desu | you shouldn't (do) | expression |
| [verb た] + ほう が いい です よ | [ta-form] hou ga ii desu yo | you should (friendly advice) | expression |
| [verb た] + ほう が よろしい です | [ta-form] hou ga yoroshii desu | you should (formal / respectful) | expression |

:::tip When to Use Which? ほう が いい vs たら どう
- **〜た ほう が いい** — Strong recommendation or advice ("you really should do X; otherwise things might turn out badly"). Adding **よ** softens the tone into friendly guidance.
- **〜たら どう ですか** — Gentle suggestion ("how about doing X?"). Better suited when you want to offer an idea rather than direct advice.
:::

## Pattern 1: Affirmative Advice (〜た ほう が いい)

**[Verb た-form] + ほう が いい (です)**

**Meaning:** "You should...", "It's better to [verb]"

Attach **ほう が いい** to the past plain [ta-form](/docs/lessons/conjugation/past/ta-form) of the verb. Literally, it means "the direction/choice of having done [X] is good."

| Usecase | Formation |
|------|-----------|
| Verb (Affirmative) | [verb た-form] + ほう が いい (です) |
| Noun | [noun] + の + ほう が いい (です) |

:::tip Why the た-form?
Even though the advice applies to future action, Japanese uses the past **た-form**. This conceptualizes the advised action as already chosen and compared against alternatives — "having done X is the better path."
:::

### Examples

- はやく ねた ほう が いい です よ。 — You should go to bed early.
  - *hayaku neta hou ga ii desu yo.*
- みず を たくさん のんだ ほう が いい です。 — You should drink a lot of water.
  - *mizu o takusan nonda hou ga ii desu.*
- いしゃ に いった ほう が いい です よ。 — You should go see a doctor.
  - *isha ni itta hou ga ii desu yo.*
- まいにち れんしゅう した ほう が いい です。 — You should practice every day.
  - *mainichi renshuu shita hou ga ii desu.*
- でんしゃ の ほう が いい です。 — The train is a better choice.
  - *densha no hou ga ii desu.*

:::caution Caution with Superiors
Because **〜た ほう が いい** implies that the speaker knows what is best, it can sound patronizing if used directly toward superiors or teachers. Toward superiors, use humble/polite alternatives like **〜た ほう が よろしい です** or ask a question like **〜て は いかが でしょう か**.
:::

## Pattern 2: Negative Advice (〜ない ほう が いい)

**[Verb ない-form] + ほう が いい (です)**

**Meaning:** "You should not...", "It's better not to [verb]"

To advise *against* doing something, attach **ほう が いい** directly to the present negative [nai-form](/docs/lessons/conjugation/present/nai-form). (Do NOT use the past negative *〜なかった*).

| Usecase | Formation |
|------|-----------|
| Verb (Negative) | [verb ない-form] + ほう が いい (です) |

### Examples

- あまり おさけ を のまない ほう が いい です よ。 — You shouldn't drink too much alcohol.
  - *amari osake o nomanai hou ga ii desu yo.*
- よる おそく たべない ほう が いい です。 — It is better not to eat late at night.
  - *yoru osoku tabenai hou ga ii desu.*
- むり を しない ほう が いい です よ。 — You shouldn't overdo it.
  - *muri o shinai hou ga ii desu yo.*
- そこ に はいらない ほう が いい です。 — You shouldn't enter there.
  - *soko ni hairanai hou ga ii desu.*
- しんぱい しない ほう が いい です よ。 — You shouldn't worry so much.
  - *shinpai shinai hou ga ii desu yo.*

## Politeness Levels

| Formality | Affirmative | Negative |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Casual | いった ほう が いい | いかない ほう が いい |
| Polite (Standard) | いった ほう が いい です | いかない ほう が いい です |
| Polite (Friendly) | いった ほう が いい です よ | いかない ほう が いい です よ |
| Formal / Business | いった ほう が よろしい です | いかない ほう が よろしい です |

## Related Vocabulary: Giving and Receiving Advice

When talking about *giving*, *receiving*, or *asking for* advice (アドバイス), pair it with giving and receiving verbs:

| Hiragana | Kanji | Romaji | English | Type |
|----------|-------|--------|---------|------|
| あげる | 上げる | ageru | to give (speaker → other person) | verb |
| くれる | 呉れる | kureru | to give (other person → speaker) | verb |
| わたす | 渡す | watasu | to hand over / pass | verb |
| もらう | 貰う | morau | to receive (from someone) | verb |

:::tip Which verb to use
**Give (あたえる側 — the giver)**
- **あげる** — I/someone gives to another person: アドバイス を **あげました** (I gave them advice)
- **くれる** — someone gives to me/my side: アドバイス を **くれました** (They gave me advice)
- **わたす** — physically hand over to someone: しりょう を **わたしました** (I handed over the document)
- **さしあげる** — humble あげる, giving to a superior: せんせい に プレゼント を **さしあげました** (I gave a gift to the teacher)

**Receive (もらう側 — the receiver)**
- **もらう** — I receive from someone: アドバイス を **もらいました** (I received advice)
- **いただく** — humble もらう, receiving from a superior: せんせい に アドバイス を **いただきました** (I received advice from the teacher)
:::
- せんせい に アドバイス を もらった ほう が いい です よ。 — You should get advice from your teacher.
  - *sensei ni adobaisu o moratta hou ga ii desu yo.*
- ともだち に アドバイス を あげました。 — I gave advice to my friend.
  - *tomodachi ni adobaisu o agemashita.*
- せんせい が いい アドバイス を くれました。 — The teacher gave me good advice.
  - *sensei ga ii adobaisu o kuremashita.*
