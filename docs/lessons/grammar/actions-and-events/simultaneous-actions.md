# Simultaneous Actions: ながら

Learn how to say you are doing two things **at the same time** — "while doing X, (do) Y" — using **ながら**.

## Key Expression

| Hiragana | Romaji | English | Type |
|----------|--------|---------|------|
| [verb ますstem] + ながら | nagara | while doing | expression |

## How It Works

**[Verb ますstem] + ながら + [Main Action]**

Take the **ますstem** (the verb without ます), add **ながら**, and follow with the main action. Both actions are done by the **same person**.

| Verb | ますstem | ながら-form |
|------|---------|------------|
| きく (listen) | きき | きき**ながら** |
| のむ (drink) | のみ | のみ**ながら** |
| みる (watch) | み | み**ながら** |
| する (do) | し | し**ながら** |

## Examples

- おんがくを ききながら、べんきょうします。 — I study while listening to music.
  - *ongaku o kiki nagara, benkyou shimasu.*
- テレビを みながら、ごはんを たべます。 — I eat while watching TV.
  - *terebi o mi nagara, gohan o tabemasu.*
- コーヒーを のみながら、しんぶんを よみます。 — I read the newspaper while drinking coffee.
  - *koohii o nomi nagara, shinbun o yomimasu.*
- あるきながら、はなしましょう。 — Let's talk while walking.
  - *aruki nagara, hanashimashou.*

:::tip The main action comes second
In おんがくを **ききながら** べんきょうする, studying is what you're really doing — listening to music is the background activity. Whatever follows ながら is the point of the sentence.
:::

:::warning ながら vs 間に
Both can translate as "while," but they are not interchangeable:
- **ながら** — **one person** doing **two actions** at once: テレビを みながら たべる (*I eat while watching TV*).
- **間に** — an action that happens **within a span of time**, often with a **different subject**: わたしが ねている あいだに、ねこが でかけた (*while I was sleeping, the cat went out*).

See [Timing Actions](./sequential-actions) for 間 / 間に.
:::

:::info ながら can also mean "although"
With certain words, **ながら** expresses contrast — "although / even though":
- しりながら、いわなかった。 — Although I knew, I didn't say anything.
  - *shiri nagara, iwanakatta.*

This use is more literary.
:::
